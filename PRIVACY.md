# Taisetsu Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Taisetsu is a private, local-first app for remembering important days. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## Information Taisetsu handles

Taisetsu lets you create information such as important-day titles, dates, notes, categories, tags, recurrence rules, reminder settings, and display preferences. This information is provided by you and is used only to deliver the app's features.

Taisetsu does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking technologies. The developer does not operate a server that receives your important-day data.

## Storage and iCloud

Your information is stored on your device. If iCloud is available for Taisetsu, Apple may synchronize the data through your private iCloud database so it is available on your devices. The developer cannot view or access the contents of your private iCloud database. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy.

Taisetsu stores a minimal display snapshot in its App Group container so its widgets can show the important days you choose. This snapshot remains within the app's local shared container and is not sent to the developer.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, Taisetsu asks for notification permission and schedules notifications on your device. Reminder content is not sent to a developer-operated server.

## Calendar access

Calendar access is optional. Taisetsu requests access only when you choose to enable calendar synchronization or export an important day. When authorized, the app creates and maintains a dedicated Taisetsu calendar and its managed events. Calendar information is processed on your device and is not sent to the developer.

## Data sharing and tracking

Taisetsu does not sell your information, share it with data brokers, track you across apps or websites, or use it for targeted advertising.

Apple may process information necessary to provide the App Store, iCloud, notifications, and operating-system services. Those services are governed by Apple's own policies.

## Retention and deletion

You can edit or delete important days in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes locally stored app data from that device. Data synchronized through iCloud may remain in your private iCloud account and on your other devices until you delete it there or remove it using Apple's iCloud data-management tools.

## Children's privacy

Taisetsu is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service.

## Changes to this policy

If Taisetsu's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the relevant app version is released. The effective date above will reflect the latest revision.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact: [dyzdyz010@gmail.com](mailto:dyzdyz010@gmail.com)

# Taisetsu 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 1 日

Taisetsu（重要日）是一款注重隐私、以本地存储为优先的重要日应用。本政策说明你在使用 Taisetsu 时，相关信息如何被处理。

## Taisetsu 处理的信息

你可以在 Taisetsu 中创建重要日名称、日期、备注、分类、标签、重复规则、提醒设置与显示偏好。这些信息由你主动提供，仅用于实现应用功能。

Taisetsu 不要求注册账户，不包含广告、第三方分析或跨应用追踪技术。开发者不运营用于接收重要日数据的服务器。

## 本地存储与 iCloud

你的信息存储在设备上。当 Taisetsu 可以使用 iCloud 时，Apple 可能通过你的私人 iCloud 数据库同步数据，以便你在自己的设备间使用。开发者无法查看或访问你私人 iCloud 数据库中的内容。Apple 对 iCloud 数据的处理受 Apple 自身条款与隐私政策约束。

为了让桌面小组件显示你选择的重要日，Taisetsu 会在 App Group 本地共享容器中保存最小化的展示快照。该快照仅保留在应用的本地共享容器内，不会发送给开发者。

## 通知

当你启用提醒时，Taisetsu 会请求通知权限，并在设备本地安排通知。提醒内容不会发送至开发者运营的服务器。

## 日历访问

日历访问是可选功能。只有当你主动启用日历同步或导出重要日时，Taisetsu 才会请求日历权限。获得授权后，应用会创建并维护专属的 Taisetsu 日历及其管理的事件。日历信息仅在设备上处理，不会发送给开发者。

## 数据共享与追踪

Taisetsu 不会出售你的信息，不会向数据经纪商共享信息，不会跨 App 或网站追踪你，也不会将信息用于定向广告。

Apple 可能会处理提供 App Store、iCloud、通知和操作系统服务所必需的信息；这些服务受 Apple 自身政策约束。

## 保留与删除

你可以随时在应用内编辑或删除重要日。卸载应用会移除当前设备上的本地应用数据。通过 iCloud 同步的数据可能继续保留在你的私人 iCloud 账户或其他设备中，直至你在那里删除，或使用 Apple 提供的 iCloud 数据管理工具移除。

## 儿童隐私

Taisetsu 是一款面向一般用户的工具类应用，不会通过开发者运营的服务主动收集儿童个人信息。

## 政策变更

如果 Taisetsu 的数据处理方式发生变化，本政策将在相关版本发布前更新，并通过上方生效日期反映最新修订时间。
